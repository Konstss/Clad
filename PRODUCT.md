# GNT Group — Product Context

## Company
GNT Group is the world's leading manufacturer of plant-based food and beverage colors. Founded in 1978 in Aachen, Germany. Headquartered in Mierlo, Netherlands. Independent, family-owned company.

## Brand
- **Name:** GNT Group
- **Product brand:** EXBERRY®
- **Tagline:** Natural Color Pioneer Since 1978
- **Tone:** Premium, trustworthy, natural, innovative, sustainable. Professional yet approachable.
- **Anti-references:** Avoid overly corporate/sterile look; avoid synthetic/chemical feel; avoid cluttered layouts

## Users
- Food & beverage manufacturers (B2B)
- R&D teams looking for clean-label color solutions
- Procurement managers at large food companies
- Sustainability officers

## Core Value Propositions
1. 100% plant-based, non-GMO colors from fruits, vegetables, plants
2. Clean label — ingredient, not additive in many markets
3. 400+ colors across the full spectrum
4. Physical processing only (no organic solvents)
5. 2,000+ customers in 75+ markets worldwide
6. Sustainability commitment (2030 goals)
7. Family-owned = long-term thinking and reliability

## Key Numbers
- Founded: 1978
- 400+ color products
- 2,000+ customers
- 75+ markets
- Offices on 4 continents

## Applications
Beverages, confectionery, bakery, dairy, snacks, plant-based meat, pet food, supplements

## Strategic Principles
- Lead with nature: everything ties back to plants
- Show the full color spectrum — it is both product and identity
- Sustainability is not a footnote, it's core
- Premium positioning without arrogance
